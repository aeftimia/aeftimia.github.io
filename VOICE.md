# Notes on my voice

- I don't use semicolons.
- Introducing examples or lists with a sentence followed by a colon feels unnatural in my prose. Work them into the sentence instead.
- Use an Oxford comma. Write “I did x, y, and z” without a colon.
- Avoid unnecessary conditionals and contrastive framing such as “X rather than Y.” State the point directly without introducing an alternative that doesn't need to be discussed.

Before sharing new or revised prose, scan it for these patterns and revise unnecessary instances. Preserve meaningful comparisons and qualifications.
