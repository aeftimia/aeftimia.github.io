# Frontier 2040 — facts and forecasts

Research notes as of **2026-10-19**. A record of public claims, not an endorsement or a prediction that they will happen. Keep the **original forecast** separate from any scenario adjustment. This page lives in a public GitHub Pages repository.

## Scenario convention (not an empirical law)

- Baseline: **end of 2026**.
- Simplifying premise for fictional worldbuilding: assume attributed predictions eventually occur, except where plainly impossible or contradicted; evaluate each separately later.
- Apply a **2× multiplier to time remaining after 2026**, not to the calendar year.
- Formula: `adjusted_year = 2026 + 2 * (original_target_year - 2026)`.
- Illustrations: 2027 → 2028; 2028 → 2030; 2031 → 2036; 2036 → 2046; 2055 → 2084.
- Dates already past or ranges without credible original deadlines **cannot be consistently transformed** by this rule.
- The ~1.8–2× historical delay figures discussed earlier are from independently maintained prediction trackers and may be subject to selection/censoring bias. They should not be interpreted as demonstrated performance for emerging science.

## Additional claims needing closer primary-source verification

- **Orbital AI compute:** Musk discussed large-scale solar-powered computing satellites and possible gigawatt annual deployment rates. Specific 2027–2030 dates and GW figures in our earlier discussion were not adequately established from a primary document; **do not treat those as verified milestones** until the original technical proposal or filing and its exact units are checked.
- **10 billion and 100 billion robots:** We discussed 2041 and 2046 target dates, but did not establish an independently verified primary quote. Excluded from dated ledger pending source review.
- **Work becomes optional / universal high income:** Musk has advocated such outcomes without a reliably established date in the material reviewed. No adjusted year.
- **Neuralink conceptual telepathy / thought-to-thought AI communication:** Long-term ambition; no defensible target date for generalized bidirectional semantic transfer.
- **First human Mars landing:** Musk has repeatedly moved near-term targets. Earlier 2024/2025 claims should be recorded with their **original forecast dates**, not reframed as fresh predictions made at end-2026.
- **Independent delay trackers:** [MuskOmeter](https://www.muskometer.com/) and [Two Weeks](https://twoweeks.lol/) report different rates and denominators. Prior chat referenced ~2× and ~1.8×, but methods and live metrics need independent checking before citing numerical failure rates.

## Observations distinct from forecasts

- Human Neuralink trials have demonstrated decoding motor intentions for cursor/keyboard-like computer control; some participants have operated software and robotic manipulators. This does **not** amount to general thought-reading.
- Noland Arbaugh (first recipient) has discussed learning cursor control and a loss of performance following movement of implanted threads: [WIRED interview](https://www.wired.com/story/neuralink-first-patient-interview-noland-arbaugh-elon-musk/).
- Neuralink's participant updates describe robotic-arm use and other activities: [Two Years of Telepathy](https://neuralink.com/updates/two-years-of-telepathy/). Company-selected anecdotes, not population averages.
- Brad Smith, an ALS patient, has publicly discussed AI-assisted drafting; using a decoded cursor/keyboard to interact with an LLM is **not** a direct neural semantic exchange: [his website](https://www.alscyborg.com/what-i-am-doing).
- A technological demonstration, market availability, mass adoption, and societal transformation are separate milestones.

## Research discipline

For every added claim record: **speaker, date uttered, exact or careful paraphrased wording, original source URL, original deadline, confidence in attribution, current status as of a specified date, and unresolved ambiguity**. Avoid quote marks around paraphrases. Treat forecasts as evidence of what someone *predicted*, not of what *will* happen.
